## 🌟 Overview  

Welcome to **PlantSnap+**, your intelligent green companion! 🌍  
Whether you're a 🌱 gardening beginner or a 🌿 pro botanist, this AI-powered tool helps you:  

- 🔍 **Identify** plant species from images  
- 🦠 **Detect** leaf & stem diseases  
- 💡 **Revive** your green buddies with smart care tips  

Just snap, upload, and let the AI do the rest! 📷

## 🔍 Features  

### 🧠 AI-Powered Identification  
Upload plant images and get instant results on species and health status.

### 🦠 Disease Diagnosis  
Detect:
- Fungal infections 🍄  
- Pest attacks 🐛  
- Nutrient deficiencies 💀  
- Environmental stress 🌡️  

### 📚 Plant Encyclopedia  
Discover detailed insights including:
- Names (common & scientific)  
- Native region  
- Light & water requirements  
- Growth tips and tricks  

### 🩺 Personalized Care  
Receive expert guidance:
- Custom care schedules  
- DIY treatment tips  
- Seasonal advice 🌤️❄️🌧️
